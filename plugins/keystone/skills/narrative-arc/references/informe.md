# Type: executive report / status review

## Order
The decision needed → what changed since last time → the evidence → options with consequences → recommendation → next steps with owners and dates.

## Scenes
`thesis` for the decision, `projection`/`bars` for evidence, `comparison` or `matrix` for options, `timeline` for next steps.

## Rules
- The first slide answers "what do you need from me?".
- Results, not process; the iteration story goes to an appendix or the repo.
