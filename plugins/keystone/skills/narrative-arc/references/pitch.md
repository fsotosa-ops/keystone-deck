# Type: investor pitch

Learned from real seed decks (IRLY, Beelinguapp, Airbyte) and from decks that raised or won with this method.

## Order (12-15 slides) and the scene for each

| # | Slide | Scene | What makes it land |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cover with the product | `cover` | The promise in one line; the product already in a phone |
| 2 | Meet the person | `persona` | A named person, a concrete day, the product in their hands |
| 3 | Problem with your own evidence | `journey` | A population that sets out and only part arrives; your own count, not a press quote |
| 4 | Solution in mirror | `mirror` | Each cause of the problem gets its answer, one per click |
| 5 | Product in context | `product` | The live product, driven click by click; callouts read its live numbers |
| 6 | Why now | `why-now` | Two changes, side by side |
| 7 | Competition | `matrix` | Two axes; you alone in a quadrant; never a feature checklist |
| 8 | Business model | `model` | Free side vs paid side, each with the screen they see |
| 9 | Market | `funnel` | The payer's budget, not users × price |
| 10 | Traction | `timeline` | "How we did it": from what there was to what there is |
| 11 | Numbers | `projection` | Cost vs income per period, the gap labelled |
| 12 | Team | `team` | Why these people, in one line each |
| 13 | The ask | `ask` | Amount, runway, use of funds slice by slice |
| 14 | Close | `closing` | The person from slide 2, resolved |

Appendix after `# Appendix`: unit economics, assumptions, risks, roadmap.

## Rules
- Kicker with the section, headline with the thesis (never "Market", "Team").
- Traction before revenue is usage evidence; say plainly what has not been tested.
- Every figure from the ledger; illustrative figures say so at the foot.
- One product, shown working, beats three screenshots.
