# Type: keynote / talk

## Order
Hook from a lived moment → the tension the audience recognises → the turn (what most people get wrong) → three ideas, each with a story and a demo or figure → what to do on Monday → the line people repeat.

## Scenes
`html` for the hook (one image, one line), `journey` or `figures` for the tension, `mirror` for the turn, `product` or video for demos, `closing` for the line.

## Rules
- Fewer words than a pitch: one sentence per slide is common.
- Interaction (a poll, a show of hands) belongs in the talk, noted in talk.md.
- The audience leaves with one sentence; write it first, as the Thread.
